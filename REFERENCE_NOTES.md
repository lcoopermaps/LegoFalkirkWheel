# Reference notes

These notes document what was used to shape the first procedural build. They are not a claim that the prototype exactly reconstructs every book illustration or every adaptation.

## Official Moominhouse material

**Moomin official — “THE MOOMINHOUSE” activity/competition PDF (2026)**  
https://assets.moomin.com/uploads/2026/03/26_Moominhouse_Competition_Updated_ENG_fix_compressed.pdf

Useful details:
- the house is presented as tall and round;
- the official cutaway shows several stacked living levels plus the roof;
- the house is described as having grown to accommodate friends and relatives;
- the cutaway supports the idea of a busy, layered interior rather than one sparse room per floor.

**Official Moomin Shop — Moominhouse building-block model**  
https://shop.moomin.com/products/moominhouse-building-block-model

Useful details:
- the product describes a three-layer staggered house with six small scenes;
- named scenes include an attic, study, kitchen, Moomin's room, living room and dining room.

## Public community descriptions used only as secondary orientation

**Moomin Wiki — Moominhouse**  
https://moomin.fandom.com/wiki/Moominhouse

Paraphrased orientation used:
- salon: fireplace with decorative plates, central table, grandfather clock, sofa, cabinet, small pictures/mirror, chandelier;
- dining room: large table, stove and cabinet;
- kitchen: stove, counter/utilities, washing corner, central table, butter churn/barrel, plate shelf;
- second floor: guest rooms and Moominpappa/Moominmamma bedroom;
- third floor: Moominpappa's office/study and guest rooms;
- roof: Moomintroll's bedroom with bed, desk and exterior roof-window route.

## Deliberate prototype inventions

The exact coordinates, room dimensions, furniture scale, forest density, jetty shape, distant Fillyjonk house exterior, NPC dialogue, map proportions and NPC routes are original implementation choices made for playability.

The prototype also intentionally avoids reproducing any copyrighted illustration, episode frame, book passage, exact production model or texture.
